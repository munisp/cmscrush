Six Mandatory Hallucination-Control Gates

G1

Span Requirement

No verbatim supporting span from the source document → no code recommendation.

G2

Vocabulary Constraint

Output restricted to valid, active codes in the approved programme version.

G3

Citation Requirement

Every finding must cite a specific rule, guideline, and chart location.

G4

Disagreement Routing

Mismatches between independent models trigger mandatory human review.

G5

Calibrated Abstention

"Insufficient documentation" is a first-class output for low-confidence cases.

G6

Symmetry Check

Both unsupported codes and missed codes must be reported symmetrically.
