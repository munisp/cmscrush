01 · The operating principle
Healthcare integrity requires evidence, not scores.
CRUSH turns fragmented claims, enrollment, provider-network, clinical-document, and beneficiary-contact signals into reviewable, point-in-time evidence.

AI can

Prioritize · explain · abstain

Surface anomalies, extract source spans, rank cases, reveal graph patterns, summarize approved guidance, and state when documentation is insufficient.

AI cannot

Decide · revoke · deny

Models never autonomously impose adverse action, alter enrollment, suspend payment, contact beneficiaries, or execute a downstream financial command.
