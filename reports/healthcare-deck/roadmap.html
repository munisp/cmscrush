Plane 09 · Governance & Roadmap

Governance Makes the Platform Defensible

Governance Pillars

Human-in-the-Loop

Temporal workflows enforce reviewer identity, rationale, and statutory timers. No adverse action without human disposition.

Fairness & Calibration

Evidently and NannyML monitor slice-level drift and disparate impact. SHAP provides evidence-based explainability.

Immutable Provenance

Every decision is hash-linked to the exact model version, feature set, and point-in-time graph snapshot.

Delivery Roadmap

Model Evaluation

Benchmark LightGBM and GNNs on CMS SynPUF and historical provider utilization data before production release.

Shadow Mode Deployment

Run AI models in parallel with existing rules to validate precision at review capacity and false-positive burden.

Financial Boundaries

Integrate TigerBeetle and Mojaloop as downstream accounting/settlement controls behind separate authorization gates.

Core Safety Invariant: AI prioritizes and explains; authorized people and policy-controlled workflows decide.
